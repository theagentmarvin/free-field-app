# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Encouraged: demonstrating empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate. Report violations privately to the maintainers (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the contact route). All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and reporters' privacy is respected.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban). This text is an adaptation of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
